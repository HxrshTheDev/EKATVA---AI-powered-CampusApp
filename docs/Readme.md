🚀 EKATVA — AI-Powered Campus Operating System

«The Operating System for Student Life
Transforming chaos into clarity through AI, prediction, and unified systems.»

---

🧠 Overview

Ekatva is a full-stack AI-powered campus super app designed to unify academic management, career development, social collaboration, and student wellbeing into a single intelligent platform.

Unlike traditional tools, Ekatva doesn’t just display data — it guides decisions.

---

⚠️ Problem

Students today operate in fragmented environments:

- Multiple disconnected platforms (LMS, WhatsApp, portals)
- No centralized academic or career planning
- No predictive insights
- High stress, missed deadlines, poor outcomes

«Students don’t lack tools — they lack systems.»

---

💡 Solution

Ekatva introduces a Campus Operating System that:

- Centralizes all student activities
- Uses AI to generate daily decisions
- Predicts academic and career outcomes
- Encourages consistent behavior through gamification

---

🧬 Core Innovation — Student Digital Twin

Ekatva builds a real-time digital model of each student.

Inputs:

- Attendance
- Study behavior
- Assignments
- Activity patterns
- Career progress

Outputs:

- 📊 GPA Prediction
- ⚠️ Burnout Risk Detection
- 📉 Attendance Alerts
- 💼 Placement Readiness Score

«“At current pace, your GPA will drop — here’s how to fix it.”»
